Research notes

Emerging tech, by sector.

Crisp field notes on the technologies reshaping climate research, with citations from leading universities and labs.

01 · Weather Foundation Models

Weather foundation models are becoming the new forecasting stack

From GraphCast and Aurora to ClimaX, data-driven models now match or beat classical NWP on key scores, at a fraction of the compute.

02 · Aerospace & Satellites

Earth observation is moving from sensors to foundation models

Multi-modal EO models from Berkeley, NASA–IBM, and Copernicus-scale pretraining are making satellite analysis transferable across missions.

03 · Materials

Low-carbon materials research is rewriting cement and concrete

Stanford and Berkeley work on volcanic clinker, recycled concrete, and abatement cost curves shows materials science is now a climate lever.

04 · Energy Systems

Long-duration storage is becoming the quiet center of clean grids

Stanford and Berkeley grid models show when multi-day storage, hydrogen, and firm clean power actually earn their keep.

05 · Manufacturing

Advanced manufacturing is becoming a decarbonization toolkit

NSF and university work on electrification, process substitution, and factory data is turning manufacturing into a climate research domain.

06 · Built Environment

Whole-life carbon is replacing energy-only building metrics

Stanford CIFE and Berkeley-linked building research show embodied carbon now rivals operations as grids clean up.

07 · Mobility

EV scale-up is constrained by finance and charging reliability

Wharton and Harvard research show that loans and broken chargers, not only battery chemistry, shape electric mobility adoption.

08 · Industrial Processes

Hard-to-abate industry is shifting from pilots to playbooks

Stanford and Berkeley research on steel, cement, heat, and abatement costs is turning industrial decarbonization into transferable methods.
