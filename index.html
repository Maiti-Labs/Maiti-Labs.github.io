Research

Methods people can use.

Climate research only matters when it can be reached, understood, and applied. We make methods, models, and findings available to researchers, communities, and decision-makers.

Our work spans space compute, weather foundation models, aerospace systems, materials science, energy, and manufacturing. In every domain, the aim is the same: climate research, made accessible.
