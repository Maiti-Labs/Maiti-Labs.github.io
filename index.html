Maiti Labs is a research organization studying the climate impact of emerging technologies. We measure how breakthroughs reshape the carbon budget, from energy systems to artificial intelligence, and deep-dive every sector where innovation remakes the planet.

  • A breakthrough only counts if the planet can afford it
    Approach

    Climate is not a footnote to innovation. It is the constraint that decides whether innovation is progress.
  • Measurement
    Pillar

    Life-cycle methods, energy accounting, and open datasets that quantify the climate load of models, machines, materials, and infrastructure.

  • Foresight
    Pillar

    Scenario research on how emerging tech scales: what demand it creates, what emissions it avoids, and which pathways stay inside planetary limits.

  • Transition
    Pillar

    Evidence for builders and policymakers: where technology accelerates decarbonization, and where it quietly works against it.

Our planet deserves research equal to the technologies remaking it. We make the climate signature of emerging technology visible, before the impacts are irreversible.
