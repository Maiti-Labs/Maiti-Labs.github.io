Research

We ask what technology costs the climate.

Emerging systems leave a climate signature in energy, land, water, minerals, and emissions. Maiti Labs makes that signature visible.

We publish open, rigorous work on how breakthroughs reshape the carbon budget: what accelerates transition, what locks in demand, and where the tradeoffs remain unclear.

Measurement

Life-cycle methods, energy accounting, and open datasets for models, machines, materials, and infrastructure.

Foresight

Scenarios for scale: demand created, emissions avoided, and pathways within planetary limits.

Transition

Evidence for builders and policymakers on where technology accelerates or undermines decarbonization.
